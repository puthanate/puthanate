# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The pain is now a moment, not a slope.** A parent falls ill before durian harvest and the heirs in Bangkok know nobody. Sharp, emotional and money-at-stake; far stronger than earlier rounds.
- **Dream outcome and speed score high.** Keep the land and its income without learning to farm; a crew leader on site in 48 hours with daily photos in LINE. Effort for the heir is near zero.
- **The payer is now named:** the heir, who has city income and the most to lose.
- **A real guarantee** (48 hours or fees refunded) attacks the likelihood problem directly.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **It is insurance, and people underbuy insurance.** The must-have moment is rare and unpredictable. Every month it does not happen, ฿990–2,500 buys a weekly report, and the lens predicts churn ("nothing happened, why am I paying?"). How often parents become unable to work is unknown, so neither demand nor the claim rate can be judged yet.
2. **Likelihood has no proof.** The heir is asked to trust a stranger crew leader with the family's most valuable harvest, using a "farm will" the parents must agree to dictate. If parents resist ("I'm not dying") the product cannot be built, and there is no case study yet.
3. **The stack is too wide for the budget.** Cover, will, hub and verified lots are several businesses. The hub (฿500–700k) is a cost base the heir does not value directly; it could consume the money before the core offer is proven.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How many heirs in your own network would pay a deposit today, before any hub exists?
- How often does a sudden handover happen per 100 farms per year? (Open; no one has this number. /founder-consumer for heir willingness, plus local records.)
- What does one lost durian harvest cost a typical farm? Without this the price cannot be anchored to value. (/founder-cfo)
- What do heirs do today: a cousin, a middleman who advances cash, a farm manager? (/founder-competitors)
- Could you sell the farm will alone at ฿15,000–30,000 first?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Narrow to one offer:** farm will plus Continuity Cover. Park the hub and verified lots until heirs have paid.
2. **Hit the stated 30-day test:** 5 refundable ฿3,000 deposits from 15–20 heirs, plus 2 signed crew leaders.
3. **Give normal months a reason to pay:** make the weekly report (income, harvest, inputs) worth the fee on its own, or price the will one-off and the cover low.
4. **Anchor price to the cost of a lost harvest** once /founder-cfo has a number; if that loss is small, this lens passes.
