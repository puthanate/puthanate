# Offers lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The dream outcome is clear:** keep the family land, live off it, never think about it.
- **Effort for the buyer is low.** The heir keeps the title and the final say, and gets plain-Thai LINE reports.
- **It does not compete on price.** The premium position is right for this lens, and sealed weighing plus honest reports in bad years answer the buyer's biggest fear: being cheated.
- **A guarantee is half-built.** What the company controls (work on time, honest numbers, fast response) can become a real guarantee.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The market may be too small and too hard to reach.** Nobody knows how many Bangkok-based heirs of Surat Thani farms exist or how to find them beyond word of mouth. A weak market is this lens's most common reason to pass. Open question: number of target heirs and cost to acquire one (/founder-consumer, /founder-competitors).
2. **Likelihood is unproven.** There is no proof yet that yield goes up or leakage goes down, the founder has no farming know-how, and only one grower family is committed. The buyer pays ฿40,000, ฿3,000 a month and an unknown installation cost on faith. Open question: real uplift and installation cost per plot (/founder-cfo).
3. **Time to result is long.** The heir sees the real result only at harvest while paying monthly. Weak time plus weak proof makes "no" easy.

Note: heirs buy comfort and trust, not AI agents. Sell the outcome; keep the technology behind it.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How many heirs fit this profile, and where would you meet the first ten?
- What would the pilot plot need to show, and by when, before a stranger would believe it?
- What can the heir see in the first 30 days that proves this is working?
- What will you guarantee on the things you control, and what happens (refund, fee waived) if you miss it?
- Could the Land Health Check stand alone as a paid product that proves value before the monthly fee starts?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Count and reach the market: an estimate of target heirs and a tested way to reach them, with the cost per heir (/founder-consumer, /founder-competitors).
2. Prove the pilot: one documented harvest season with sealed-weighing numbers against the baseline.
3. Add a guarantee on what the company controls, costed by /founder-cfo.
4. Give the heir a visible result within 30 days (the Health Check report and baseline), and a known first-year total including installation.
5. At least two grower partners outside the founder's partner's family willing to sign.
