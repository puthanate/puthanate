# Monopoly lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The small market is the right shape.** Absentee heirs of farmland in a single province is a tight, nameable group. Taking a big share of that group is a better place to start than a thin slice of "Thai agritech".
- **There is a candidate secret.** Most of the loss may happen *at the handover*: short-weighing, grade switching, side-selling by insiders. Most agritech watches the crop, not the hand-off. If that is true, the sealed scale plus the expected-yield model goes after money the owner never sees disappearing. This is unverified.
- **The data could compound.** Every plot adds yield, tree-count and crew-score history. Over time that could make the expected-yield model and the vetted-crew network hard to copy locally.
- **Brand fits the positioning.** "The heir's side of the table" can become a durable brand where trust is the product.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No proprietary technology.** Drones, free satellite imagery, certified scales, cameras and LINE are all off the shelf. Nothing here is ten times better on its own. A local drone operator, a farm-management firm or a durian operator could put together the same stack in month six. The only defensible parts (yield data, crew network) do not exist yet.
2. **Distribution is untested.** Lawyers, Land Office, banks, cooperatives and Bangkok heir networks are all plausible channels. None has produced a customer, and the cost of acquiring one is unknown. At ฿40,000 upfront plus ฿3,000/month, a slow or costly channel sinks a sub-฿1M budget.
3. **The team lacks the domain half of the know-how.** The expected-yield model is the core of the "see" layer, and it needs agronomy the founder openly rents. If the model's baseline is wrong, alerts are noise and trust is lost. Durability then rests on partners who could serve a competitor just as easily.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How many target heirs exist in Surat Thani, and who already sells monitoring or caretaking to them? (/founder-competitors)
- What leakage do owners actually lose per rai, per crop, and is it more than ฿36,000 a year plus the check? (/founder-consumer, /founder-cfo)
- What does the IoT/scale install cost per plot, and what does each customer cost to acquire through each channel? (/founder-cfo)
- What do you know about handover theft that a well-funded local operator does not?
- Why now? What has changed (EU traceability, heir demographics, cheap drones) that makes this possible today and not five years ago?
- In ten years, what stops a buying yard or a cooperative offering the same sealed scale for free?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Prove the secret.** Measure handover leakage on 5–10 real plots and show a gap big enough to justify the price.
2. **Prove one channel.** Land 10 paying heirs through a single channel and know the cost of each.
3. **Name the moat.** Write down which one will be built (crew network, yield data or brand) and the milestone that shows it is working. Then lock in an agronomy partner on terms that are exclusive within the province.
