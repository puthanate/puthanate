# Surat Smart Land (working name)

- What it is: Farm Continuity Cover for Surat Thani durian and rubber families. The heirs pay a monthly fee so that the family farm never stops, even when the parents can. The company keeps an up-to-date "farm will" (the parents' know-how, routines, crews, buyers and boundaries), and if a parent suddenly can't work, a vetted local crew leader is on site within 48 hours to run the farm for up to 90 days. It is delivered through a shared village hub for 30–40 neighbouring farms (pooled inputs, peak-season crew dispatch, a monthly agronomist round, sealed weighing).
- Who it is for: the payer is the 2nd/3rd generation children (often in Bangkok) of parents who still farm durian and rubber in Surat Thani. The children don't know how to farm and don't want to, want to keep the land and its income, and value comfort and trust. The parents are users and also benefit from the hub.
- What it sells, at what price (estimates, to be tested):
  - Continuity Cover: about ฿990–2,500 per farm per month, paid by the heir. Covers the farm will, crew standby and the weekly LINE report.
  - Activation: crew wages are paid from the farm's own revenue, plus a 10–15% management fee on revenue during the activation.
  - Service guarantee (not a crop guarantee): crew leader on site within 48 hours, or fees refunded.
  - Farm will as a one-off product: ฿15,000–30,000.
  - Hub revenue:
    - an 8–12% margin on pooled fertiliser and fungicide orders
    - ฿300–500 per crew-day booked in peak weeks
    - 1–2% on harvest weighed at the hub and sold in bulk
  - Later: buyers (ล้ง / packing houses) pay about ฿1–2/kg for verified durian lots with logged maturity dates, a chain of custody and GAP done.
- Where and how: one cluster of 30–40 farms around the pilot family farm (the founder's partner's family, durian and rubber) in Surat Thani. Heirs reached through family networks, school alumni and hometown LINE groups in Bangkok. Delivered on LINE. A certified scale, a corner of an existing shed, and a part-time agronomist.
- Budget and constraints:
  - Bootstrapped, under ฿1M, founder(s) only. Year-1 hub estimate is ฿500–700k.
  - The founder has technology skills, not farming know-how. Know-how comes from the parents, local crew leaders and an agronomist.
  - No guarantee on crops, weather or prices.
  - The long-term vision of an AI agent per farm job is parked until the core works.
