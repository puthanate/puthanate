# Surat Smart Land (working name)

- What it is: smart farming support for farm families in transition. While the parents still farm, the company equips them with smart farming tools (sensors, weather and water alerts, crop calendar, disease warnings, harvest records) that help them decide better. At the same time, it records the farm's know-how, routines and history. When the land passes to the children who don't want to farm, the farm continues as a documented, reported asset, run by trusted grower partners, and the heir receives income and an honest LINE report.
- Who it is for: farm families in Surat Thani growing durian and rubber (later oil palm and others), where the parents still farm and the 2nd/3rd generation children (often in Bangkok) will inherit but don't know how to farm and don't want to. The children want to keep the land and live off its income, with comfort and trust. The parents want to keep the farm going and pass it on well.
- What it sells, at what price (premium, not competing on price; amounts carried over from earlier rounds, to be retested):
  - Farm set-up and Land Health Check: ฿40,000 one-off.
  - Smart farming equipment installation: charged to the family. Cost per plot not yet known.
  - Monthly service: ฿3,000 per plot per month.
  - After the handover: a performance share on yield above the baseline (percentage not yet set).
  - No guarantee of crop results or income.
- Where and how: Surat Thani first. The pilot is a real family durian and rubber farm the founder has a personal connection to. Families reached through the children (Bangkok networks) and the parents' local networks. Delivered through LINE.
- Budget and constraints:
  - Bootstrapped, under ฿1M, founder(s) only. The founder has technology skills, not farming know-how.
  - Farming know-how comes from the parents themselves, grower partners and an agronomist advisor.
  - Tools recommend and people decide.
  - The family keeps the land and the final decisions.
  - Long-term vision: an AI agent for each farm job (weather, water, crop calendar, plant health, harvest, market, crew, paperwork), built in stages.
