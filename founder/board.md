Nobody passed, and nobody would fund it as pitched today. The pain is real and specific. The proof, the focus and the moat are not there yet.

## Risks more than one member raised

1. **No proof yet that the money is there.** All three lenses: nobody has measured how much heirs actually lose to short-weighing, side-selling and theft. Without that number, the buyer is asked to trust a team without farming know-how, and the price can't be justified.
2. **The yield model depends on rented know-how.** Monopoly and Product: the "expected yield" baseline is the brain of the product. If it's wrong, alerts are noise and trust collapses. The agronomy partner could just as easily serve a competitor.
3. **Unproven reach and unknown size.** Offers and Monopoly: lawyers, the Land Office, banks and co-ops are plausible channels, but none has produced a customer, and the cost to acquire one is unknown. On a budget under ฿1M, a slow channel is fatal.
4. **Price against value on small plots.** Offers and Product: ฿40k up front plus ฿36k a year plus IoT may cost more than a small rubber or palm plot loses.
5. **Too many products at once.** Product, with Offers asking for "one narrow offer": eight components across five crops is too much for founders alone.

## Where they disagree

- **Where the moat lies.** Monopoly says off-the-shelf tech is no moat, and the defensible things are data, the crew network and brand, none of which exist yet. Offers is less worried about a moat and more about whether buyers believe it works. Product says the moat is *trust in the data*: one wrong alert loses it.
- **How much to cut.** Product wants a single crop and a single promise, deferring marketplace, voting, playbooks and the statement. Offers would keep the boundary survey and Land Statement in the first 30 days as quick visible wins.

## Conditions checklist (for the rest of the pack)

- [ ] **Measure real leakage** on 3–10 plots (free or at cost) and turn the result into a case study. *(Offers, Monopoly)*
- [ ] **Pick one crop and one promise** to launch, likely durian ("see every harvest, weighed and sealed"). Defer marketplace, voting and playbooks. *(Product, Offers)*
- [ ] **Find the minimum plot size and crop** where the fee costs less than the loss it stops. → `/founder-cfo`
- [ ] **Get the IoT and scale install cost per plot** and check it against the price. → `/founder-cfo`
- [ ] **Confirm heirs will pay** the Health Check price for visibility alone, before building hardware. → `/founder-consumer`
- [ ] **Count target heirs and map who already serves them.** → `/founder-competitors`
- [ ] **Prove one channel:** 10 paying heirs through a single channel, with cost per customer known. *(Monopoly)*
- [ ] **Map every hand-off** from harvest to the LINE message, make each one tamper-evident, and test it on real plots. *(Product)*
- [ ] **Write down yield-model accuracy per crop**, and only send alerts that would stand up if a sibling questioned them. *(Product, Monopoly)*
- [ ] **Add an outcome guarantee**, e.g. the Health Check fee credited back if no recoverable loss is found. *(Offers)*
- [ ] **Deliver a visible result within 30 days:** survey, tree count, statement. *(Offers)*
- [ ] **Name the moat** (crew network, yield data or brand) with a milestone, and lock in an agronomy partner exclusive to the province. *(Monopoly)*
- [ ] **Answer "why now?":** EUDR, heir demographics, cheap drones. *(Monopoly)*

## The strongest version the board can see

This is narrower than what was pitched. **A sealed-harvest service for absentee durian owners in Surat Thani:** a certified scale, camera and tags at the handover, and one trusted LINE message per harvest showing weighed against expected, with photos. It is sold with a paid audit that guarantees to find a baht figure or refund the fee. Durian comes first because the value per rai is highest, so the fee is small next to the possible loss, and handover leakage is most painful. Rubber and palm come later only if the numbers work at their lower value per rai. The family voting, Land Statement, playbooks and crew marketplace are a strong *second act*, unlocked once the sealed-harvest data has earned trust and gives the scored crew network something to stand on. In short, the board would fund a narrow "you'll finally see every kilo" business, not yet the full platform.
